The **Library** shows every photo and video, newest first, in rows that keep each photo's own shape. Scroll down and press **Load more** to see further back.

## Narrowing it down

- **Filters** down the left: keywords, rating, colour label, pick or reject, year, camera, lens, place, creator, file type, where the original is kept, landscape or portrait, with or without GPS, and more. Each shows how many photos match. Choose as many as you like; **Clear all** starts again.
- **People:** **With people**, or how many (one, 2–5, 6 or more), or **Without people**. Anyone who can be seen counts, even far off in a crowd or a landscape. See [People in photos](/help/ai-review).
- **Keywords:** type in **Find a keyword** to find one in a long list. The most used show first; **Show all** shows the rest.
- **The timeline** across the top shows how many photos you have per month. Click a month to see just those.
- **Sort** by **Date taken** or **Recently added**.
- **Search** with the box at the top of every page. See [Searching](/help/search).
- **Saved searches:** press **Save this search** in the filters to keep the current filters and search words under a name, at the top of the filters.

The page address carries your filters, so you can bookmark a view or send it to a colleague who also has access.

## Looking closer

Click a photo to open it large. Use the arrow keys to move through the photos, **Z** to zoom, and **I** to show its details. **Open details** takes you to the photo's own page, where you can edit everything about it (see [A photo's page](/help/photo-page)).

## Rating, labels and picks

With a photo open, or selected in the grid:

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **1** to **5** | Star rating |
| **6** to **9** | Colour label (red, yellow, green, blue; purple from the menu) |
| **P** | Mark as a pick |
| **X** | Mark as a reject |
| **U** | Clear pick or reject |
| **← →** | Previous or next photo |
| **Z** | Zoom |
| **I** | Show or hide details |
| **Esc** | Close |

In the grid, the arrow keys move between photos, **Enter** opens one, **Space** selects it, **Shift+A** selects everything shown, and **Esc** clears the selection.

## Working on many photos at once

Select photos with **⌘-click** (Mac) or **Ctrl-click** (Windows), or **Shift-click** to select a run. A bar appears with what you can do to all of them at once:

- rate, label, pick or reject;
- add keywords (**Tag**);
- **Add to album…**;
- apply a **Preset…** (fill empty fields, or overwrite them): see [Presets](/help/presets);
- **AI…**: analyse them again, or use the AI's alt text, captions or keywords where they're empty: see [AI review](/help/ai-review);
- **Compare** two to four photos side by side (**Z** zooms all of them together);
- **Delete**.

What you can do depends on your role: see [People and roles](/help/people-and-roles).

## The map

**Map** (above the photos) shows where your photos were taken, for photos with GPS. The library's filters carry over, so you can map just one keyword or one year.

## Making it fit your screen

The library uses the whole width of your window. Choose **small**, **medium** or **large** thumbnails under **Account → Appearance** (see [Appearance and accessibility](/help/appearance)).
