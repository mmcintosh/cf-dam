cf-dam should be comfortable for everyone to use, including people with low vision, colour blindness, motion sensitivity or who use a keyboard or screen reader. Your choices are kept with your account, so they follow you to any computer you sign in on.

## Language

cf-dam speaks English, Spanish, German, French and Italian. Choose yours from the **language list at the bottom of the menu**, or under **Account → Language**, where **Same as my browser** follows your browser's own language. The choice is kept with your account.

Your photos' details, keywords and albums stay as they were written. AI suggestions are written in the language set under **AI review**.

## Light or dark

The **sun / moon button** at the bottom of the menu switches between light and dark at once. Dark suits looking at photos; light can be easier for reading and in bright rooms.

Under **Account → Appearance → Theme** you can also choose **Same as this computer**, which follows your computer's own light or dark setting.

## Appearance settings

On **Account**, under **Appearance**, each change shows straight away:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Text size** | Normal, Larger, Large or Largest (up to 150%). Everything grows with it and the page reflows, so nothing is cut off. |
| **Library thumbnails** | Small, Medium or Large photos in the library. |
| **Stronger contrast** | Black or white text, firmer edges and thicker focus outlines. |
| **Easier-to-read font** | Atkinson Hyperlegible, designed by the Braille Institute for people with low vision: letters that are easy to tell apart (I, l and 1; O and 0). |
| **Underline links** | Links are underlined everywhere, so they don't depend on colour. |
| **No animations** | Turns off movement and transitions. (They're already kept to a minimum if your computer asks for less motion.) |

**Back to the usual** resets them all.

Your browser's own zoom (**Ctrl** or **⌘** and **+**) works too, alongside the text size.

## Using a keyboard

- **Tab** moves through links and buttons; a clear outline shows where you are.
- The first **Tab** on any page offers **Skip to content**, which jumps past the menu.
- **⌘K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows) jumps to search.
- The library has keys for moving, opening, rating and labelling photos: see [The library](/help/library).

## Screen readers

Buttons and fields are labelled, pages are marked up with headings and landmarks, and every photo has text a screen reader can read: its alt text if it has one, otherwise its title or file name. [AI review](/help/ai-review) can draft alt text for photos that have none.

## Standards

These choices follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.2), which the US Section 508 rules and the European accessibility rules build on: text that can be enlarged, stronger contrast, no motion when asked, links not told apart by colour alone, and everything usable from a keyboard. If something in cf-dam is hard for you to use, please tell your administrator, or report it to cf-dam's makers, so it can be fixed.
