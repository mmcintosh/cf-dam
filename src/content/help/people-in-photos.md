**People** in the menu lists everyone you've named in your photos, with a photo of them and how many photos they're in. Names are for you and your team: **clients never see them**, in galleries or anywhere else.

Naming is done by hand, like keywords. cf-dam doesn't recognise faces.

## Naming someone

- **On a photo's page**, under **People**, type a name in **Add a name** and press **Add**. A name you've used before is offered as you type; a new one makes a new person. **×** beside a name takes it off the photo.
- **On many photos at once**: select them in the Library, type the name in **Name a person…** in the bar, and press **Add** (or **Take off**).
- **On People**, **Add a person** makes someone with no photos yet.

The same name however it's typed (*anna hansen*, *Anna  Hansen*) is the same person.

## Finding them

- On People, open someone and press **See all their photos**.
- In the Library, **Named people** in the filters lists the people in what's shown, the most photographed first. Choose two or more to see photos with all of them together.
- Search finds names too: typing *Anna* finds photos she's named in.
- A saved search keeps the people chosen.

Naming someone in a photo whose **People** count said nobody was there changes it to one person, unless someone set the count by hand.

## Names already in your files

If you named people in Lightroom (its People view) or another program that writes **PersonInImage** or face region names into the file's metadata, cf-dam reads those names when the photo is uploaded or catalogued, and tags the photo with them. They're marked **from the file** on the photo's page. Photos already in cf-dam are read again when they're re-sent.

## Changing and removing

On a person's page you can:

- change their **Name** (a name someone else already has is refused: merge them instead), add **Notes**, and mark them as **A child (under 18)**;
- **Merge** them with someone else who's the same person: their photos move over and this name goes;
- **Delete this person**: the name comes off every photo it's on, including photos in the trash.

Naming, merging and deleting need permission to name people (administrators and editors by default). Viewers see the names on photos they can see. Activity records that a person was added, changed, merged or deleted, but not who, so a deleted name doesn't linger there.
