An **album** is a set of photos you choose by hand: a wedding, a magazine story, a portfolio, the shortlist for a client. A photo can be in any number of albums, and adding it to one doesn't move or copy it.

## Making an album

On **Albums**, type a name in **New album name** and press **Create album**.

## Adding photos

- **Many at once:** select photos in the [library](/help/library) and choose **Add to album…**.
- **One at a time:** on a [photo's page](/help/photo-page), under **Albums**, choose one and press **Add**.

The Albums page shows each album with its cover (the first photo, unless you choose one), and how many items are in it. Open one to see its photos.

## Renaming, the cover, taking photos out

At the top of an album:

- **Rename** changes its name and its description (the line under the name).
- **Remove photos or choose the cover…** shows the album's photos with a tick box on each. Tick the ones to take out and press **Take … out of the album**. Tick just one and press **Make it the cover** to show it on the Albums page. Taking a photo out of an album never deletes it: it stays in your library and any other albums.
- A photo's own page also lists its albums: **×** beside one takes it out of that album.

## Deleting an album

**Delete album** asks first, on the page. Only the album goes: every photo in it stays in your library. **Any client gallery links made from it stop working**, and the favourites clients made in them go too, so check **Client galleries** first if a client may still be choosing. It can't be undone, and it's recorded in [Activity](/help/alerts-and-activity).

## Sharing an album with a client

An album is also what you share with a client: open it and use **Share with clients** to make a private link with its own password, expiry date and download choices. See [Client galleries](/help/client-galleries).

Removing a photo from cf-dam (see [Duplicates](/help/duplicates), or **Delete** in the library) takes it out of every album too.
