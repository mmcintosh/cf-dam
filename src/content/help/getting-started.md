cf-dam is a private archive for your photos (and videos). It keeps a catalogue of everything you have: what each photo is, when and where it was taken, who's in it, which keywords it has, and where the original file is kept. You can then find any photo in seconds, share some with a client, and keep the originals safe in storage that suits you.

It runs in **your own Cloudflare account**. Nobody else can see your photos unless you share them.

## How it's laid out

The menu on the left takes you to each part:

| Menu | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Library** | Every photo, newest first, with filters down the side. |
| **Albums** | Photos you've grouped by hand. |
| **Client work** | Jobs, clients and client galleries, with a tab for each along the top. Client galleries are albums shared through a private link. |
| **Tidy up** | **Duplicates** (identical files and look-alikes, with a suggestion of which to keep), **Keywords** (rename, merge and tidy your keyword list) and **Presets** (ready-made credit, copyright and contact details to add to photos). |
| **Setup** | **Storage** (where original files are kept: Cloudflare's own storage, Amazon S3 and others), **Computers and NAS** (small helpers that catalogue photos on your NAS or computer without moving them) and **AI review** (captions, alt text and keyword suggestions written by AI, for you to approve). |
| **Team** | **People** (who can sign in, and what each person may do), **Activity** (what was done, by whom) and **Alerts** (problems that need someone; the number beside **Team** is how many are open). |
| **Account** | Your password, passkeys, two-step sign-in and how cf-dam looks for you. |

Some of these only show for administrators. Every page has a **?** beside its title that opens the help for that page.

## Three ways to bring photos in

1. **Upload them.** Drag photos (or whole folders) onto the library. Good for a few hundred at a time. See [Uploading photos](/help/uploading).
2. **Catalogue them where they are.** If your photos live on a NAS or a computer, a small helper program reads them there and sends cf-dam their details and previews. The originals don't move. See [Computers and NAS](/help/computers-and-nas).
3. **Catalogue a storage bucket.** If your photos are already in Amazon S3 or similar, connect it on the Storage page and import what's there. See [Where originals are kept](/help/storage).

## First things to do

1. **Make your sign-in safer.** Add a passkey or turn on two-step sign-in on the Account page. See [Your account and sign-in](/help/account-security).
2. **Bring in some photos** by any of the three ways above.
3. **Let AI describe them** (optional, a few cents per hundred photos). See [AI review](/help/ai-review).
4. **Try a search.** Type a word in the search box at the top of any page, or describe what you're looking for. See [Searching](/help/search).
5. **Make it comfortable to use.** Light or dark, larger text, stronger contrast: see [Appearance and accessibility](/help/appearance).

> **New to words like "original", "preview", "bucket" or "IPTC"?** The [glossary](/help/glossary) explains them in plain words.
