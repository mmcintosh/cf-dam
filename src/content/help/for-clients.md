Your photographer uses cf-dam to share photos with you. With your own account you see only the galleries they've shared with you, from any phone, tablet or computer.

## Signing in

Your photographer sent you an invite link. Open it, choose a password (at least 12 characters; a few words you'll remember work well), and you're in. Each invite works once, for two weeks; if yours has expired, ask your photographer for a new one.

Next time, open the same web address, sign in with your email and that password, and you go straight to your photos.

If you forget your password, ask your photographer: they can give you a temporary one, and you choose a new one when you sign in.

## Your galleries

**Your galleries** shows everything shared with you, newest first, and how long each one stays open. When there's just one, signing in takes you straight to it. Closed galleries stay on the list, faded, so you know where they went; ask your photographer if you still need them.

## Looking through your photos

- Click or tap a photo to see it large. Use the **‹ ›** arrows, the arrow keys, or swipe, to move through them; **Esc** or **✕** closes it.
- **♥** marks a favourite. **♥ Favourites** shows only those.
- When you've chosen, press **I'm done**. Your photographer sees your selection as finished. You can still change it afterwards; they'll see that too.

Your favourites are kept with your account, so you can start on your phone and finish on your computer.

## Downloading

If your photographer allows downloads, the large view has a **⤓** button for each size you may have: **Small** or **Large** JPEGs for sharing and printing, or the **Original file**. "Original: ask the photographer" means that file isn't kept online; they can send it to you.

## Your account

**Account** has your password, a **passkey** (sign in with your fingerprint, face or phone instead of a password), two-step sign-in if you'd like it, where you're signed in, and **Appearance**: light or dark, larger text and other ways to make the pages easier to see. See the sun/moon button at the bottom of the menu for light or dark.

**Sign out** is at the bottom of the menu, or at the top of a gallery.
