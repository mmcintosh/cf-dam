Everyone who signs in to cf-dam has their own account, and a **role** that decides what they can do.

**Clients** are different: they see only the client galleries you invite them to, nothing else. You invite them from an album's **Share with clients**, not from here, and they're listed apart, under **Clients**, at the bottom of **People**. A client can also just be sent a link, without an account. See [Client galleries](/help/client-galleries).

## Adding someone

On **People**, press **Add person**, and give their **Name**, **Email**, a **Temporary password** and a **Role**. Press **Create**, then tell them the address of your cf-dam and the temporary password (in person or by phone is safest). The first time they sign in, they're asked to choose their own password.

## Two-step sign-in for everyone

At the top of **People**, **Two-factor sign-in → Required for** can be **Nobody**, **Administrators** or **Everyone on the team** (clients are never made to, though they can turn it on themselves). People who must have it and don't are asked to set up a passkey or an authenticator app before they can do anything else. **Everyone on the team** is the safest choice. See [Your account and sign-in](/help/account-security).

## Helping someone

Press **Manage** beside a person:

- **Temporary password:** **Set** a new one if they've forgotten theirs. It signs them out; they choose their own at the next sign-in. It also lifts a password lock.
- **Lost phone or passkey:** turns off their two-step sign-in codes (and optionally removes their passkeys), and signs them out. They set them up again on **Account**.
- **Locked out:** after too many wrong passwords an account locks for a while. It lifts by itself; **Unlock now** lifts it straight away.
- **Sign out everywhere:** signs them out of every browser (for a lost laptop, say).
- **Deactivate:** stops them signing in, and keeps the account for later. **Reactivate** lets them back in.
- **Delete:** removes the person and their sign-ins. Photos, albums and galleries they made stay.

Your own password, passkeys and sign-ins are on **Account**, not here.

Everything people do that matters (sign-ins, changes to people and settings, deletions, shares) is recorded on **Activity**: see [Alerts and activity](/help/alerts-and-activity).
