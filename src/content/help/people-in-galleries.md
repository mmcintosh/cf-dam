A wedding gallery can hold hundreds of photos. A gallery can let **the couple** find the ones they want: all the photos of Grandma, of the bride's family, of the kids. They choose a name above the photos, and the gallery shows only the photos with that person or group in it. Choosing two shows photos with both.

It uses the names you give people in your photos (see [People in photos](/help/people-in-photos)). Nobody is recognised automatically.

## Who sees names

Only **the gallery's client's own people**, and only when they're signed in:

- The gallery's album must be in one of the client's **jobs**; that's how cf-dam knows whose gallery it is.
- They must be **invited** to the gallery with an account, and listed under **People with a login** on the client's page.
- Anyone else invited (a planner, a guest) sees the gallery without names. So does anyone with just the link; when the gallery shows people, they're asked to sign in first, and nothing names anyone.

Nothing about people is shown until you turn on **Show people** for that gallery.

## Setting it up

Open the gallery (**Picks and stats** under its link, or **People**), and find **People in this gallery**:

1. Turn on **Show people to the invited people of** the client.
2. Make the list. **Add a group** adds one of the client's groups. **Suggest from these photos** lists everyone tagged on the gallery's photos: tick who to add. People named only in the files start unticked.
3. For each entry, set the **name the couple sees**. It starts as the first name, so "Anna Smith" shows as "Anna"; change it to "Mum" or "Grandma Rose" as you like.
4. **Save the list.** **What the couple sees** then shows the names and counts exactly as they'll get them, and what's listed but not shown, with the reason.

## Groups

A group is a name and some people: "Bride's family", "Groom's family", "Bridal party", "The kids". Groups belong to the client, so every gallery for that client can use them. Make them on the client's page, under **Groups of people**, or in a gallery's people settings, under the client's groups.

Choosing a group shows photos with **anyone** in it.

## Children

Children (people marked **A child (under 18)**) are handled carefully:

- By default a child has **no name of their own** in a gallery. Their photos are found through groups, such as "The kids". **Show children's names** turns that off for one gallery.
- A group with a child in it is shown only when at least two of its people in the gallery have no name of their own. Otherwise, with the other names beside it, the group would point to a single child, and it's held back.
- This makes a single child hard to pick out, not impossible: someone who knows the family can still recognise them in the photos.

**Is this a child?** in the suggestions changes the person everywhere, not only in that gallery.

## Never show to clients

On a person's page, **Never show to clients** leaves them out of every gallery's people, whatever its settings. They're never counted in a group either: a group shows only because of the people in it who can be shown.

## Names from the couple

The couple often know who's who better than you do. When **Let them suggest names** is on (it comes on with **Show people** the first time), the photo viewer in their gallery has a **Who's in this photo?** button. They can tick one of the names they already see, or type a name, for that photo and any others they tick.

Nothing is tagged when they do. Their suggestions wait under **Names to check** on the gallery's page, and the list of client galleries shows how many are waiting. They're grouped by name, so "Aunt Mary" on twelve photos is one decision:

- **Is this…** offers who it might be, people listed in this gallery and the client's own first. Someone of another client is shown with that client's name and never chosen for you.
- **A new person** makes them, linked to this client, so erasing the client with their named people removes them too. If someone already has that name, you're told who, and can choose them or use a different name.
- Untick the photos that aren't them: those keep waiting. **Reject** drops the ticked ones.
- **Also show them in this gallery** lists them, so the couple get a name to find them by.
- To decide "Aunt Mary", "Auntie Mary" and "Mary" as one person, tick the box beside each name first.

Accepted names are tagged like any other, marked "suggested by the client" on the photo. Rejected ones are deleted, and the couple aren't told. Only the couple can suggest, and only their own logins see what's waiting. Names to check still works after a gallery closes. If the couple stop being the gallery's client (the album moves to another client's job, or their login is unlinked), what they suggested disappears.

If email is set up, you hear at most once an hour per gallery how many names are waiting. The email never contains the names.

## Before you share

The settings warn about two things:

- A **name shown** for a group that names a child or someone never shown, like "Lily's friends".
- Photos whose **title, caption or alt text** names someone tagged on them. Every visitor sees those, names or not.

Something for your contract or privacy notice: the couple will see the names you list, and can find every photo those people are in. If they can suggest names, they may also give you the names of their guests, children included.
