A **preset** is a named set of the credit, rights and place fields you'd otherwise type again and again: your copyright notice, credit line, usage terms, contact email and website, or a client's job details.

## Making one

1. Open **Tidy up → Presets** in the menu and press **New preset**.
2. Give it a **Preset name**, such as "My copyright" or "Hansen wedding 2026".
3. Fill in only the fields you want it to set. **Only filled-in fields are part of the preset**: empty ones are left alone when it's applied.
4. Tick **Apply to new uploads** to fill these fields on every new photo where the file left them empty. A copyright preset is the classic use.
5. Press **Save preset**.

**Edit** changes a preset later; the change applies to photos it's applied to from then on.

## Applying a preset

- **To one photo:** on its page, under **Credit, rights & place**, choose **Apply preset (fill empty fields)…**.
- **To many photos:** select them in the [library](/help/library) and choose **Preset…**, then either:
  - **fill empty fields**: keeps whatever is already there, and fills the gaps; or
  - **overwrite**: replaces existing values with the preset's.

Fill empty fields is the safe choice: nothing anyone typed is lost.

## Which fields?

The same ones as on a photo's page: headline, creator and job title, credit line, source, copyright notice, usage terms, copyright URL, email, phone, website, sublocation, city, state or province, country and country code, job identifier and instructions. These are the standard IPTC fields that agencies, newspapers, Lightroom and Photo Mechanic read. See the [glossary](/help/glossary).
