The search box is at the top of every page (press **⌘K** on a Mac or **Ctrl+K** on Windows to jump to it). There are two kinds of search.

## Searching for words

Type one or more words and press Enter. cf-dam finds photos where **every** word appears in any of:

- the file name (`IMG_2041-final` is found by `2041` or `final`);
- the title and caption;
- keywords, **including the ones above them and their synonyms**: a photo keyworded *Bergen* (under *Places › Norway*) is found by `norway`;
- the camera and lens;
- the place: city, state, country, sublocation;
- what [AI review](/help/ai-review) saw, and any text it read in the picture (a sign, a document);
- credit, creator, source and job details.

Words match from their start: `heron` finds *herons*, `port` finds *portrait*. The search combines with any filters you've chosen, so you can search within one year or one keyword.

## Search by meaning

Sometimes nobody wrote down the word you're thinking of. **Search by meaning** looks for photos whose descriptions *mean* the same thing, in other words:

- `bird flying over water` finds a gull's caption that says *in mid-flight above the sea*;
- `small bird perched on a branch` finds hummingbirds and finches;
- it works in other languages too: `pájaro en el agua` finds the water birds.

After a word search, press **Search by meaning** under the title to try it. The results come nearest first, and **Search the words instead** takes you back.

Every photo's page also has **More like this**, which finds photos with similar descriptions.

### What it needs

Search by meaning reads the **words about a photo** (title, caption, keywords, place and the AI's description), not the pixels. A photo with nothing written about it can't be found this way, so it works best once [AI review](/help/ai-review) has described your photos. New and changed photos are added to it within a minute or two.

It's switched on by your administrator: if you don't see **Search by meaning**, it isn't set up on your archive. (Setup is one command, described in cf-dam's README.)

## Saving a search

Press **Save this search** in the library's filters to keep the current search words and filters under a name. Saved searches appear at the top of the filters, with how many photos match.
