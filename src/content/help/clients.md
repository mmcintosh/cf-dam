**Clients** keeps a record of each person or company you work for: a couple, a family, a business. Each client has a page with their contact details and notes, the people who sign in for them, the client galleries shared with those people, what they downloaded, and a history of what happened.

Only **administrators** see Clients: these are people's personal details.

## Adding a client

On **Clients**, press **New client**. A name is enough (for example *Anna and Tom Hansen* or *Brücke GmbH*); you can add the company, email, phone, address, the language for their galleries and notes on their page. Notes are for your team only.

The list shows current clients A to Z, with how many logins they have, how many of their galleries are open, and when one of them last opened or downloaded something. Search by name, company, email or phone, or by the name or email of one of their people. Accents don't matter: *muller* finds *Müller*.

## Their people

A client can have several people who sign in: a couple, or an agency's art buyers. Under **People with a login** on the client's page:

- **Add a person** gives their name and email. If they don't have a login yet, one is made and you get a one-time **invite link** to send them (cf-dam sends no email). They choose their own password with it.
- **Link an existing login** picks someone you already invited to a gallery. Logins with the client's email come first.
- **New invite link** makes a fresh link for someone who hasn't joined yet; the old one stops working.
- **Unlink** takes someone off the client. Their login and their galleries stay.

A login belongs to one client at most. People on your team can't be linked to a client.

Linking someone **doesn't give them access to anything**. Each gallery is still shared with people one by one: open the gallery and invite them under **Clients with an account** (see [Client galleries](/help/client-galleries)). That way, an agency's art buyers don't see each other's galleries.

## Jobs

Each piece of work for the client, such as a wedding or a portrait session, is a **job**, listed on their page with its shoot date and stage. **New job** makes one for this client. See [Jobs](/help/jobs).

## Galleries, downloads and history

The client's page lists every gallery shared with any of their people or made from one of their jobs' albums, with whether it's open, when it closes, and its opens, favourites and downloads. **Downloads** shows what they downloaded, by whom, in which size and from which gallery. **History** shows what was done to the client and their logins: added, changed, invited, joined, signed in.

On **People**, the **Clients** tab shows which client each login belongs to.

## Past clients

**Mark as past client** hides a client from the list once the work is done; nothing is deleted. **Past clients** at the top of the list shows them, and **Make current again** brings one back.

## Erasing a client

If a client asks you to delete their data, **Erase client** at the bottom of their page removes:

- their details and notes;
- their logins, with their passwords, sessions and invites;
- the names and emails they gave in galleries (their favourites stay, marked *(erased)*);
- their jobs, and their details in the history.

Galleries, albums and photos stay. Type the client's name to confirm. This can't be undone, though backups made before it still hold the data until they expire (see [Backups](/help/backups)).
