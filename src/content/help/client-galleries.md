A **client gallery** shows one album to someone outside your team: a couple choosing their wedding photos, an art director picking shots, a family downloading prints. You send them a link; they see only that album, nothing else in your archive.

## Making a link

The quickest way: select photos in the **Library** and choose **Share with a client…**. An album is made for them (rename it on its page) and the gallery form opens. Or, on **Client galleries**, press **New client gallery** and choose an album. Step by step:

1. Make an album with the photos to share (see [Albums](/help/albums)) and open it.
2. Under **Share with clients**, press **New link**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Who it's for (only you see this):** for example "Anna and Tom".
   - **Gallery title** and **Message to the client**: what they see at the top.
   - **Password (optional):** they'll need it to open the gallery. Send it separately from the link (a text message, say).
   - **Open until (optional):** after this date the link stops working.
   - **Downloads:** which sizes they may download, if any (small JPEG, large JPEG, the original).
   - **Watermark**: show your watermark on everything they see and download (set one up first: below).
   - **Clients can pick favourites:** turns on picking (below).
   - **Show filenames:** shows each photo's file name, handy for ordering prints by number.
   - **Final delivery** (only for an album that's part of a job): these are the finished photos. Making the link moves the job to **Delivered** (see [Jobs](/help/jobs)).
4. Press **Make the link**, then **Copy link** and send it.

One album can have several links: one per family member, say, each with its own password and downloads. **Edit** changes a link; **Revoke** (press it twice) stops it working at once.

## What the client sees

They open the link, type the password if there is one, and see the gallery: your title and message, then the photos. They can open each one large and move through them, and download the sizes you allowed.

- **Originals are never watermarked.** Originals on a NAS or in deep storage can't be downloaded from a gallery: the client sees "Original: ask the photographer".
- The gallery is plain and carries your title, not cf-dam's name, and search engines are told not to list it.

## Favourites (proofing)

With **Clients can pick favourites** on, the client taps **♥** on the photos they like. The first time, they're asked **Who's picking?**: a name (and email, if they like), so you know whose favourites they are. When they've finished, they press **I'm done**, and their favourites are sent to you.

Under **Picks and stats** on the link (or on the **Client galleries** page), **Who picked what** shows each person, their number of favourites, downloads, whether they're **still picking** or have sent, and when they were last seen. You can view the photos picked by any or all of the people you tick, and **Make an album of these** in one click, ready to edit or deliver.

## Your watermark

On **Client galleries**, under **Watermark**, either type your name or studio and **Use this text**, or upload your logo (a PNG with a transparent background works best). Choose **Where** it goes (centre, a corner, or tiled across), its **Size** and its **Strength**. **Open the gallery as a client sees it** shows the result.

The same watermark is used by every link that has it turned on, and by **Download a copy** with the watermark on a photo's page. **Remove the watermark** turns it off everywhere.

## The Client galleries page

**Client galleries** lists every link: which album, whether it's open, expired or protected by a password, how many times it was opened, how many favourites and downloads it has had. The buttons above the list show the open ones, those closing within 7 days, the expired and the revoked; the **Closes** column says when each one stops working.

## Clients with an account

A link is enough for a one-off job. For clients you'll work with again, or who'll want to come back, **invite them to an account** instead: they sign in and see only their own galleries, never the rest of your archive.

1. Make a link as above, with the downloads, watermark and expiry you want for them.
2. Under the link, press **Invite a client**, give their name and email, and press **Invite**.
3. cf-dam shows an **invite link** once. When this archive can send email (see [Email](/help/email)), **Email the invitation to them** is ticked and they get it by email, in the gallery's language; otherwise copy it into your own email or message. It works once, for two weeks, and only the newest link for a person works.
4. They open it, choose a password, and land in the gallery. Next time they sign in at your cf-dam's address.

What changes for a client with an account:

- **No gallery password.** Signed in, they skip the link's password. (Anyone else with the link still needs it.)
- **Their own favourites.** Their picks are kept with their account, on any device, and show under **Picks and stats** with their name.
- **Your galleries.** Every link you invite them to is listed for them on one page; with just one, signing in takes them straight there. A revoked link disappears from their list; an expired one shows as closed.
- **Nothing else.** The menu shows only their galleries, Account and their help page ([Your galleries](/help/for-clients)). Every other page sends them back to their galleries.

Under each link, its clients show whether they've joined. **New invite link** replaces an invite that was lost or expired (the old one stops working). **Take off this gallery** stops them seeing it; their account stays for other galleries. Clients are listed apart from your team on [People](/help/people-and-roles), where you can give them a temporary password, deactivate or delete them.

## Emails to clients

When this archive can send email (see [Email](/help/email)), clients with an account hear about their galleries:

- **Email the members**, under a link, shows the email first (with your gallery's title and message), then sends it to every client who has joined and hasn't turned these emails off. It can be sent once every six hours.
- **Closing soon**: three days before a gallery closes, clients who haven't pressed **I'm done** get a reminder. Once per closing date: if you move the date, they're reminded again.
- **Finished choosing**: when a client presses **I'm done**, the person who made the gallery gets an email (or the administrators, if that person has gone). At most once an hour per gallery.

Every email about a gallery has **Don't email me about this gallery** at the foot. Clients can turn them back on under **Your galleries**. Under each link, a client shows **emails off** when they have, and a warning when the last email to them didn't arrive: send them links yourself then. Clients reached only by a link (without an account) are never emailed.
