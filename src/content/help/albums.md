An **album** is a set of photos you choose by hand: a wedding, a magazine story, a portfolio, the shortlist for a client. A photo can be in any number of albums, and adding it to one doesn't move or copy it.

## Making an album

On **Albums**, type a name in **New album name** and press **Create album**.

## Adding photos

- **Many at once:** select photos in the [library](/help/library) and choose **Add to album…**.
- **One at a time:** on a [photo's page](/help/photo-page), use **Add to album**.

The Albums page shows each album with its first photo as a cover, and how many items are in it. Open one to see its photos.

## Sharing an album with a client

An album is also what you share with a client: open it and use **Share with clients** to make a private link with its own password, expiry date and download choices. See [Client galleries](/help/client-galleries).

Removing a photo from cf-dam (see [Duplicates](/help/duplicates), or **Delete** in the library) takes it out of every album too.
